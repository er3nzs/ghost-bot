# Privacy Policy — Ghost

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

This privacy policy explains what the Discord bot **Ghost** stores, why it stores it, and how you can request deletion.

## What we collect and store

When you use the bot's verification features, the bot stores only:

- Your Discord user ID
- The server (guild) ID where verification happened
- Your verification status and the time it happened
- The role ID the bot assigned to you

The bot does **not** read or store message content, does **not** store presence data (online status or activities), and does not collect any other personal information.

## Why we store it

The data is used only to confirm that you are a member of the server, to give or remove the verified role when you join or leave, and to remove the record of anyone who leaves.

## How long we keep it

Records are kept while you are a member of the server. When you leave, your record is deleted within 24 hours.

## Who can access it

Access is limited to the bot's operator. The data is never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never sold.

## Deletion requests

You can request deletion of your data at any time by opening an issue on this repository or by contacting the bot owner on Discord. Requests are completed within 7 days.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always the one at this URL.
